Private prayer requests that make sure no one feels unheard

Members send a prayer need straight to their pastors, privately. Pastors respond with care, and members see the answer.

Start for freeSee pricing

For members

Signed-in members of your church can ask for prayer in under a minute.

  • Write a request and choose a category such as healing, family or thanksgiving
  • Send it anonymously if you prefer; pastors will not see your name
  • Read your pastor’s reply and mark a prayer as answered when God comes through

For pastors and church admins

A calm inbox instead of messages scattered across WhatsApp.

  • See new requests, ones you are praying for, and answered prayers
  • Reply with an encouraging word or scripture
  • Branch pastors see only their own branch’s requests

Private by design

  • Requests are never shown to other members
  • Only pastors and church admins of the member’s own church can read them

Frequently asked questions

Who can see a prayer request?

Only pastors and church admins of your church (and, for a branch, that branch’s pastors). Other members cannot see it, and anonymous requests hide your name.

Do members need to be signed in?

Yes. Requests come from members connected to a church, which keeps them private and prevents spam.
