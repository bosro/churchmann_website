Frequently asked questions

Do I pay per SMS?

SMS allowances depend on your plan, and extra SMS credits can be added. See the pricing page for details.

Can a branch send its own messages?

Yes. Branch staff message their own branch, and headquarters can set a monthly SMS allowance for each branch.
