Multi-branch church management from one headquarters dashboard

Whether you have three branches or thirty, see how each is doing and stay close to your pastors, without chasing spreadsheets.

Start for freeSee pricing

See every branch at a glance

  • Compare branches by members, giving, attendance and growth
  • Spot which branches need support
  • Branch pastors see only their own branch

Run the money side

  • Set what percentage each branch remits to headquarters
  • See what is due, what was paid and the balance for the month
  • Set a monthly SMS allowance for each branch

Stay close to your pastors

  • Branch pastors submit a monthly report: attendance, new converts, baptisms and prayer points
  • See at a glance which branches have reported
  • Transfer members between branches with a clear history

Frequently asked questions

Can branch pastors see other branches?

No. Branch staff are limited to their own branch. Headquarters sees everything.
