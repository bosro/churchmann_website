Who can sign in?

  • Church admins, pastors, finance officers and leaders of your church
  • Members whose church has connected them to Churchmann, to view their giving and send prayer requests
  • Branch pastors, who see only their own branch

Need help getting in?

  • Forgot your password? Use the “Forgot password” link on the sign-in page to reset it by email
  • Invited by your church? Open the invitation email and follow the link to set up your account
  • Still stuck? Visit our Help center

Frequently asked questions

Is Churchmann free to use?

You can start on the Free plan. See the pricing page for paid plans.

Is my church data secure?

Each church sees only its own data, and private areas such as prayer requests are limited to the right leaders.
