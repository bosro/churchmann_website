Frequently asked questions

Can members sign in to Churchmann?

Yes. Members connected to your church get their own sign-in where they can view their giving and send private prayer requests to your pastors.

Can I manage more than one branch?

Yes. Branches have their own members, and headquarters can compare branches and transfer members between them.
