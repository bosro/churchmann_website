Frequently asked questions

Is Churchman free for small churches in Ghana?

Yes. There is a Free plan to get started, and paid plans add more members, messaging and features.

Can members give with mobile money?

Yes. Online giving runs through Paystack, which supports mobile money in Ghana.

Does Churchman work for churches outside Ghana?

Yes. Churchman is used by churches in Ghana and across Africa and beyond.
